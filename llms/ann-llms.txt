# ANN Inc.

> ANN Inc. is the former parent company of Ann Taylor and LOFT, specialty retailers of
> women's apparel, shoes and accessories. It was acquired by Ascena Retail Group in 2015
> and sold to Sycamore Partners in December 2020 for $540 million alongside Lane Bryant
> and Lou & Grey. The brands now operate under KnitWell Group (formerly Premium Apparel
> LLC), a Sycamore Partners holding company that also owns Talbots.
>
> ANN Inc. publishes NO first-party developer API. As of 2026-09-02 there is no developer
> portal, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL/gRPC/SOAP contract, no MCP server, no A2A agent
> card, no SDK on any public package registry, and no /.well-known document on any of its
> hosts. The only programmable surface associated with the brands is an affiliate program
> operated by third-party networks (FlexOffers, CJ Affiliate) — those networks own the
> catalog feed, link-generation and reporting APIs, not ANN Inc.
>
> This file is GENERATED by API Evangelist from the public profile at
> https://apis.io/providers/ann/. It is not published by ANN Inc.

## Brands and sites

- [Ann Taylor](https://www.anntaylor.com/): Women's apparel, shoes and accessories storefront (Salesforce Commerce Cloud).
- [LOFT](https://www.loft.com/): Sister brand storefront for casual women's apparel.
- [KnitWell Group](https://www.knitwellgroup.com/): Holding company for Ann Taylor, LOFT and Talbots under Sycamore Partners.
- [KnitWell Group Careers](https://jobs.knitwellgroup.com/): Careers site for the group.

## Programmable surface

- [Ann Taylor Affiliate Program (FlexOffers)](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/ann-taylor-affiliate-program/): Commission on sales attributed to affiliate links to anntaylor.com. The link, feed and reporting APIs are FlexOffers'/CJ Affiliate's, not Ann Taylor's; commission rates are disclosed inside the network after approval.
- [LOFT Affiliate Program (FlexOffers)](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/loft-affiliate-program/): Same shape for the LOFT brand.
